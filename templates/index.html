Price pane — moving averages
EMA 8 (cyan) · fastest moving average — reacts to short-term momentum first
EMA 21 (blue) · medium trend filter — close above = bullish bias, below = bearish
EMA 50 (coral) · longer-term trend anchor — strong support/resistance level
Pink ▼ above a bar · EMA 8 crossed BELOW EMA 21 (bearish trigger)
Green ▲ below a bar · EMA 8 crossed ABOVE EMA 21 (bullish trigger)
When price is above 8 > 21 > 50 in order: stacked bullish. Below all three: stacked bearish.
Option-chain levels (solid / long-dashed) — dealer hedge magnets from the live 0DTE chain
Max Pain (gold solid) · strike where the most options expire worthless. Strong price magnet into the close on expiration days.
Call OI (light-magenta long-dash) · strike with the heaviest call open interest — dealers short these calls are hedging gamma → acts as resistance.
Put OI (orange long-dash) · strike with the heaviest put open interest — dealers short these puts are hedging gamma → acts as support.
Call Vol · today's heaviest-volume call strike — live order flow magnet.
Put Vol · today's heaviest-volume put strike — live order flow magnet.

S1 · S2 · S3 (green) · support levels — price tends to bounce here

Stacked EMA Trend (D8 · D21 · D50)

—
Loading…

Inspired by TOS's "Stacked EMA" banner. ▮ Bullish stack = price > D8 > D21 > D50. ▮ Bearish stack = price < D8 < D21 < D50. ▮ Mixed = no clean hierarchy. The stack is the simplest and most reliable single-number trend gauge.

ADX — Trend Strength

—
Loading…

ADX measures trend strength, regardless of direction. ADX < 20 = market is ranging (mean-revert plays). ADX > 25 = strong trend (momentum plays). +DI > -DI = bullish direction; -DI > +DI = bearish direction.

TTM Squeeze — Volatility Compression

—

Bollinger Bands inside Keltner Channels = market is coiling (low volatility, big move pending). Color of momentum bars: ▮ rising positive, ▮ falling positive, ▮ rising negative, ▮ falling negative. Squeeze "fires" when BB exits KC — direction = sign of momentum.

Overnight High / Low (Premarket Range)

Loading…

The range from yesterday's 4pm ET close to today's 9:30am ET open. Day traders watch for breaks of ONH/ONL as continuation signals.

Volume Confirmation (VC)

—

Triggers when latest-bar volume exceeds 1.5× the 20-bar average AND price closes in the matching direction. A "VC: BULLISH VOLUME" tag is meaningful conviction signal.

Chandelier Exit — ATR Trailing Stop

Loading…

A widely-used trailing stop. Long-side stop = Highest High (22) – 3 × ATR (22). Short-side = Lowest Low (22) + 3 × ATR (22). Use as a hard stop for swing positions; for 0DTE, divide by 5–10 since timeframe is shorter.
